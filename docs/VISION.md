# Vision for Pro Clinic Website

Pro Clinic is a beauty and wellness salon in Billdal, Gothenburg, offering skincare, massage, medical foot care, laser treatments, lashes, brows and hairdressing. The website is the digital face of the salon and the primary tool for new and existing clients to discover services and book appointments.

## The Problem Today

Currently, potential clients cannot easily find out what treatments Pro Clinic offers, what they cost, how long they take, or who provides them. Without an effective online presence, interested visitors cannot quickly assess the salon's quality or professionalism, and the salon loses bookings and revenue.

## What Success Looks Like

The website makes treatment discovery effortless. A visitor arriving at the site immediately understands what Pro Clinic offers. They can browse treatments by category, see pricing and duration, learn about the team, and quickly move to booking. The visual design conveys luxury and professionalism without coldness or corporate stiffness; it feels warm, trustworthy, and established.

Once live, the website becomes a modern digital home for Pro Clinic. It builds trust through clear, professional information. It showcases the salon and its practitioners. It turns visitors into booking requests and, over time, into returning clients.

## Key Principles

**Clarity over decoration.** Information architecture is intuitive. Treatment categories are obvious. Calls-to-action for booking are prominent. No visitor should struggle to find what they are looking for.

**Premium without flashy.** The design feels luxurious and established, not trendy or cluttered. Photography, whitespace, and typography work together to convey quality.

**Personal connection.** The team page and about section make the salon feel like real people, not a faceless business. This personal service is what makes Pro Clinic special and must come through online.

**Easy booking initiation.** Visitors do not book directly on the site; they contact the salon to request an appointment. The website makes this as frictionless as possible.
